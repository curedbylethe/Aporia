# Editorial

Lead with the argument, decision or instruction. Use sections that answer reader questions in order and give them direct anchors on long documents. Keep the title short and put scope, date and provenance in a quieter band.

| Artifact | Opening | Evidence and navigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dossier | The problem, outcome and relevant constraint | Architecture, measured results with method, tradeoffs and retrospective |
| Proposal | Decision sought and constraints | Alternatives, reasons, dependencies and uncertainties |
| Study material | Objective and prerequisites | Contents, examples, exercises and a revealed solution |
| Field manual | Audience, scope and quick start | Ordered procedure, lookup table and exceptions |
| Résumé | Name, role and contact | Experience, outcomes and selected technical work in print order |

Paper is the default. Digital reading may expose an explicit Ink preference with preserved contrast. Print always uses Paper and suppresses interface chrome. A bounded raised sheet fits a print-like layout. Long references can use a continuous canvas. Give interactive regions Interface states and focus rules.

Each figure needs a caption on the same page. Identify screenshots, illustrative diagrams and simulations. For repeats such as a chart family, keep labels and axes consistent.
