# Aporia identity / concise brand reference

**Aporia** is the overarching identity and token architecture. **Lacuna** names its editor and terminal theme family, offered as **Lacuna Paper** and **Lacuna Ink**. Neither is a competing brand.

The palette comes from archival paper, bone, onyx and coal with restrained warm red. Paper preserves a warm document ground, while Ink is a designed dark mode—not an automatic inversion. Ochre, sage and muted utility hues support syntax and functional states without turning the brand into a rainbow.

The identity favors typography with strong hierarchy, small document-like metadata, folio numbering, measured rules and square geometry. Aporia Editorial can use grain plates, large headlines and asymmetric documentary layouts. Aporia Interface may use dense tables, conventional controls, icons, multiple surfaces and essential status colors. Lacuna must favor practical editing legibility and host conventions over decorative restrictions.

No independent hex values belong in this document. For exact colors, fonts, spacing, radii and modes, consult [`../aporia/tokens.json`](../aporia/tokens.json); for binding design rules, consult [`../aporia/RULES.md`](../aporia/RULES.md).

Red must not be the sole signifier of an error or selected state. Use words, shape and/or focus indicators as appropriate. Functional red in editor syntax or diagnostics is allowed even when an editorial composition limits decorative accents.
