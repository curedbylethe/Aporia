# Documentary voice

Put the outcome or action first. Use specific nouns, active verbs and labels a reader can recognize on the screen. Give each paragraph one job.

| Draft | Revision |
| --- | --- |
| Powerful tools for modern workflows | Filter by process name, then inspect its memory and launch evidence |
| Blazing fast | Cached lookup took 12 ms median across 50 local runs |
| This process is abandoned | The launcher exited. Two workers remain. Abandonment is unconfirmed. |
| There was a problem | Sync stopped at 3 of 12 files. The device disconnected. |

Identify fixtures, estimates, projections and unknowns where they appear. Use sentences for explanation and brief labels for navigation. Keep instructions scoped to the action that follows and avoid restating the information in a neighboring chart or heading.
