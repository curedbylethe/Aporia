# DDR-008 · Digital reading modes

Accepted in 0.2.0 · 2026-11-28

## Context

Digital readers may choose a darker ground and still need printable output.

## Alternative

A single print-derived background would ignore digital reading preferences.

## Decision

Default Editorial to Paper, allow opt-in digital Ink and use Paper for print.

## Consequences

Test print and both enabled digital presentations independently.
