# DDR-007 · Evidence-first storytelling

Accepted in 0.2.0 · 2026-11-28

## Context

Technical claims are useful when their scope can be understood.

## Alternative

Claims and simulations without provenance present uncertain results as measurements.

## Decision

Include method, units, baseline and fixture or capture labels at the point of use.

## Consequences

Golden references provide notes alongside their working artifacts.
