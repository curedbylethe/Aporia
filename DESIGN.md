# Aporia design contract

Aporia organizes information with the discipline of a technical publication and
the operating clarity of an instrument. Lacuna Paper and Lacuna Ink are its
editor and terminal theme editions.

1. Define the foremost action or argument. Establish a clear reading order with type, alignment and spacing.
2. Choose **Editorial** for reading and print, or **Interface** for operating software. Assign the mode per region on hybrid pages.
3. Use the canonical [tokens](dist/web/) and [rules](aporia/RULES.md). Paper is the editorial and print default. Ink is available for software and optional digital reading.
4. Compose with square geometry, useful density, restrained surfaces and readable text. Let red annotate a decision, exception or focal point.
5. Give measurements a source, unit and method. Identify the provenance of screenshots and label simulated evidence.
6. Keep interaction keyboard accessible, show focus and use a second cue for state. Preserve coherence in grayscale.
7. Translate the design to the medium. Terminal cells and native controls carry hierarchy through the affordances the host supports.

Start with [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for task routing and see [the working references](references/) for concrete applications.
